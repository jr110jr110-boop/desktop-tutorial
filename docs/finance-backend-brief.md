# Finance module (full accounting inside PMIS), release F1 + F2 — backend brief

Company decision (binding): **all financial operations are done in PMIS**. Sepidar only receives the data the tax office and the social security organisation need (sales invoices for the taxpayer system, VAT summary, payroll insurance list, payroll tax list). The existing cost accounting (`CostingService`, `AccountingEvent`, `AccountMapping`, `CostEntry`) stays and becomes the **sub-ledger feed** of the new general ledger. ★ = provisional architect decision; implement as configurable data, never hard-coded.

Statutory figures that change every year (VAT rate, minimum wage, allowances, income-tax brackets, insurance ceiling, depreciation rates) are **NOT seeded with invented values**. They live in dated rate tables; a setup-check endpoint lists what is missing; any operation that needs a missing rate fails with a clear Persian message. Only the few long-standing statutory ratios listed below are seeded, each with its `Source` text and `Verify = true` so finance confirms them once.

Jurisdiction: the company is preparing a move to Germany. Everything country-specific (chart of accounts, tax codes, payroll rules, calendars, report layouts, export formats) is data or a pluggable rule set keyed by `Jurisdiction` ("IR" now; "DE" later). Base currency is a company setting (IRR now). Every voucher line carries an optional foreign currency amount and rate. Do not build the German rule set now — only keep the seams.

# F1 — general ledger, treasury, receivables, tax

## 1. Fiscal years and periods
- `FnFiscalYear` (title e.g. "1405", start, end, status {Open, Closing, Closed}, opening voucher id, closing voucher id). `FnPeriod` (year, number 1..12, start, end, status {Open, Locked, Closed}). Seed helper `EnsureFiscalYearAsync(date)` creates the Persian year containing `date` with its 12 periods (idempotent; called on startup for today and when posting into a year that does not yet exist and is the next year after the latest).
- Locked: only FINANCEMANAGER can post; Closed: nobody. Reopen a Locked period: FINANCEMANAGER with reason (logged). Closed is final.
- ★ Events dated in a Locked/Closed period are posted on the first day of the first Open period with flag `LatePosting = true` and the original date kept in the line description; warehouse and procurement are never blocked by accounting periods.

## 2. Chart of accounts (کدینگ حساب‌ها)
- `FnAccount` 3 levels inside the ledger: Group (گروه, 1 digit), General (کل, 2 more digits → 3), Subsidiary (معین, 2 more digits → 5). Fields: code (unique), title, level, parentId, nature {Debit, Credit, Either}, type {Asset, Liability, Equity, Revenue, Expense, Memorandum}, `Temporary` (closed at year end: revenue, expense, cost of sales, summary), `DetailTypes` (which detail-account types are allowed / required on this subsidiary, flags), `RequiresDetail`, `CurrencyAllowed`, `StatementLine` (code of the financial-statement line it rolls into), `IsActive`, `SepidarCode` (optional, for the tax/insurance exports only), `Jurisdiction`.
- Postings only to Subsidiary level. An account with postings cannot be deleted, only deactivated; its code cannot change.
- Detail accounts (تفصیلی شناور): `FnDetailAccount` (code, title, type {Person, Supplier, Customer, Employee, Bank, CashBox, PettyCash, Project, WorkOrder, CostCenter, Asset, Other}, linked entity id (ProviderId / ContractId / WorkOrderId / CostCenterId / EmployeeId / BankAccountId / AssetId), isActive). Created automatically by the system when a provider, customer, contract, work order, cost centre, employee, bank account or asset is first used in a posting (humans never create them by hand for these types). Up to 2 detail levels per voucher line: `Detail1Id` (party/bank/employee) and `Detail2Id` (project/work order/cost centre).

## 3. Vouchers (اسناد حسابداری)
- `FnVoucher` (fiscal year, number (final, gapless within the year, assigned at finalisation), temporary number (assigned at creation), date, type {Opening, Normal, Auto, Closing, Reversal, YearEndTemporaryClose}, status {Draft, Checked, Final}, description, source type/id/number (for auto vouchers), `AccountingEventId` (nullable, unique when set), `ReversesVoucherId`, `LatePosting`, created/checked/finalised by + at, `[Timestamp]`). `FnVoucherLine` (row, accountId (subsidiary), detail1Id, detail2Id, debit, credit, currency code, currency amount, rate, quantity, description, workOrderId, contractId, costCenterId).
- Rules: debit = credit (exact) and > 0; each line has exactly one of debit/credit > 0; account must be active, Subsidiary, allow the detail types given, and `RequiresDetail` honoured; date inside an Open period of an Open year (FINANCEMANAGER may use Locked periods); currency fields together or none. Final vouchers are immutable; correction only by a Reversal voucher (created by the system from the original, then a new voucher). Draft/Checked may be edited by FINANCIAL; Check by FINANCIAL (not the creator for manual vouchers; auto vouchers may be checked by any FINANCIAL user); Finalise by FINANCEMANAGER (bulk endpoint: finalise all Checked vouchers up to a date, in date then temporary-number order — final numbers follow date order).
- Gapless final numbering per year: final number = max+1 assigned inside the finalise operation; enforce a unique index (fiscalYearId, number) and retry on conflict.
- **Auto-posting of accounting events** (the bridge from the existing sub-ledgers): a hosted service (every 5 minutes ★, offline) plus an endpoint `POST gl/post-events` converts every `AccountingEvent` with status Pending/Exported and no voucher into one Auto voucher (status Checked — the system is the preparer; FINANCEMANAGER finalises): role → mapping → account; ProviderId → detail1 (Supplier), WorkOrderId/ContractId/CostCenter → detail2. Reversal events → reversal voucher of the original event's voucher. Idempotent (unique AccountingEventId). The AccountingEvent status meaning changes: Pending = not yet posted to the PMIS ledger; add status `Posted = 4`; Sepidar no longer receives these events (the old CSV export endpoint stays for history, but the CEO dashboard "Sepidar" counters now read the new Sepidar outbox, §8).
- Every new F1/F2 posting (treasury, receivables, assets, payroll) also goes through `AccountingEvent` + roles (so there is one posting path), with new event types (30+).
- Reports (read only, paged where lists, all with fiscal year and date range filters; JSON + Excel export endpoint for each, using the existing Excel helper if any, else ClosedXML if already referenced, else CSV UTF-8 with BOM):
  - Journal (دفتر روزنامه): final vouchers by date, grouped per day, totals per General account per day (Iranian legal-book layout).
  - General ledger (دفتر کل): per General account, running balance.
  - Trial balance (تراز آزمایشی) at levels General/Subsidiary/Detail, 2/4/6 columns (opening, period turnover, closing), option include Draft/Checked.
  - Account review (مرور حساب) for an account or a detail account with running balance; detail-account statement across accounts (e.g. a supplier's or customer's statement).
  - Balance sheet and income statement from `StatementLine` (seed statement lines in Persian per Iranian Accounting Standard 1 layout ★: current assets lines, non-current assets lines, current liabilities, non-current liabilities, equity; revenue, cost of revenue, gross profit, admin/selling expenses, other operating income/expense, operating profit, finance costs, other non-operating, profit before tax, tax, net profit).
  - Project profitability: per contract revenue (60101 with detail2 project), cost (7xxx with detail2 project) and margin — ties to the existing CostEntry figures (difference listed).
  - Reconciliation check endpoint (extends the existing `CheckAsync`): ledger balance of inventory subsidiaries vs ItemCost value; GRNI ledger vs open receipts; AP ledger per supplier vs PayablesService open amounts; bank ledger vs treasury balances; unposted events count; vouchers not final older than N days.
- Year-end: `POST gl/years/{id}/close` (FINANCEMANAGER, all periods Closed or Locked, no Draft/Checked vouchers in the year, reconciliation check clean or explicitly overridden with reason): creates (1) the temporary-accounts closing voucher (revenue/expense/cost → 50901), (2) 50901 → 50301 (retained earnings), (3) closing voucher of permanent accounts (اختتامیه), (4) the next year's opening voucher (افتتاحیه) with the same balances per account and detail. Year becomes Closed.
- Opening balance sheet (first go-live): endpoint to import the opening trial balance (Excel: account code, detail code, debit, credit) into an Opening voucher of the first year; validated and balanced; FINANCEMANAGER finalises.

## 4. Treasury (خزانه‌داری)
- `FnBankAccount` (bank name, branch, account number, IBAN (شبا, validated: "IR" + 24 digits, mod-97), currency, accountId (10102/10103), detail account auto, isActive). `FnCashBox` (title, custodian job title, accountId 10101). `FnPettyCash` (holder user id, holder job title, ceiling amount, accountId 10104, detail per holder).
- `FnReceipt` (دریافت) and `FnPayment` (پرداخت) documents: number, date, party (detail: customer/supplier/employee/other), lines of instruments {Cash, BankTransfer, Cheque, Card/POS, Offset}, each line with amount, bank account / cash box, reference (transfer tracking number), cheque link; purpose links (customer invoice ids for receipts; payment request ids / supplier invoice ids / payroll run / petty-cash top-up for payments). Status {Draft, Approved, Posted, Cancelled}. Posting creates the accounting event.
- **Integration with existing PaymentRequest**: today `PayablesService` marks a request Paid and writes a Bank event. Change: TREASURY creates an `FnPayment` from one or more Approved payment requests (amount = Amount − Deductions; deductions split into typed deduction lines, §6), choosing the instrument; posting the payment marks the requests Paid (keep the old endpoint working for backward compatibility but route it through the same service so the Bank event is not duplicated). Payee bank account must be the supplier's registered IBAN for transfers (supplier profile gets `Iban`; transfer to a different IBAN needs FINANCEMANAGER approval).
- Cheques:
  - Received `FnReceivedCheque` (Sayad ID 16 digits (شناسه صیادی), serial, bank, branch, account holder, due date, amount, drawer = party, status {InBox, Deposited (در جریان وصول), Cleared, Bounced, ReturnedToDrawer, Endorsed (خرج شده to a supplier)}, status history rows with date and voucher). Postings: receive → 10302; deposit → 10303; clear → bank; bounce → 10304 (and party's account stays as debtor); endorse to supplier → AP of the supplier.
  - Guarantee cheques/promissory notes (چک و سفته تضمینی) received or given: memorandum accounts 901xx (no P&L effect), with return/collection events.
  - Issued `FnChequeBook` (bank account, series, from/to serial; leaves auto-generated) and `FnIssuedCheque` (leaf, Sayad ID, payee, due date, amount, status {Issued, Cleared, Cancelled, Voided(spoiled leaf)}). Issue → 30102 (اسناد پرداختنی); clear (from bank reconciliation or manual) → bank. A leaf cannot be used twice; voided leaves are kept in the register.
  - Due-date alerts (received cheques due in 3 days not deposited; issued cheques due in 7 days with bank balance short) on the finance dashboard and CEO dashboard.
- Petty cash: top-up = payment to the holder; settlement `FnPettyCashSettlement` (lines: expense account/cost centre/work order, amount, VAT, supplier name optional, receipt document attachment) approved by FINANCIAL (not the holder) → posting; balance per holder; ceiling enforced; the existing procurement "petty-cash purchase" receipts (receipt without PO ≤ 50M rial) appear as candidate lines.
- Bank reconciliation (مغایرت‌گیری): import a bank statement (Excel/CSV: date, description, debit, credit, reference, balance) into `FnBankStatement`/lines; auto-match by amount + date window (±3 days ★) + reference; manual match; unmatched bank lines can be turned into vouchers (bank fees → 80201, interest → 60301) by FINANCIAL; the reconciliation report (book balance, outstanding cheques, deposits in transit, bank balance, difference = 0 required to approve); approval by FINANCEMANAGER (not the preparer). Matching an issued cheque clears it.
- Cash position report: balances of every bank, cash box and petty cash; received cheques by due week; issued cheques by due week; approved payment requests not yet paid; 8-week forecast (inflows: cheques due + customer invoices due + payment milestones Due from project control; outflows: issued cheques + approved payment requests + payroll (last run total) + open PO lines by delivery date).

# F2 — fixed assets, payroll and insurance, Sepidar outbox
